Controlled ontology application benchmark

One source corpus. Two native semantic models.

H₂KG and CHAMEO were mapped independently from the same sanitized characterization manifest. It contains 8 source records across 7 method families and 7 unique profiles. The frozen requirements, expected rows, and conclusion rules were fixed before query retrieval or native-pattern assessment. Neither ontology was modified, and no local semantic bridge was admitted to either scored graph.

Computed outcome

For the selected characterization profiles, the benchmark supports a scoped complementarity conclusion: CHAMEO provides explicit characterization-methodology structures, while H₂KG provides greater native class coverage at the predefined profile granularity together with direct valued-parameter and individually provenance-linked result patterns.

The 12/13 and 10/13 values are requirement-satisfaction rates under this frozen protocol, not general ontology-accuracy or quality scores. The benchmark covers eight source records representing seven unique profiles; governance, maintainability, reasoning performance, usability, and scalability were not measured.

Quantity interpretation

Parsing is separate from scientific compatibility

The result-value requirement contains 13 records with a parseable number and recognized unit. A separate rule-based screen found 11 records without a detected property/unit conflict and 2 conflicts. The two conflicts are the duplicated source entry electrolyte saturation = 47 mol/L: mol/L denotes concentration, whereas saturation is ordinarily dimensionless. The raw entries remain queryable but are not treated as scientifically compatible or independently verified saturation quantities.
