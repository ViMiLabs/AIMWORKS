Hydrogen ontology

IRI
https://w3id.org/h2kg/hydrogen-ontology
Type
Ontology
Explore
Open in Explorer

The ontology captures hydrogen electrochemical concepts spanning PEMFC and PEMWE profiles, including parameters, properties, measurements, instruments, materials, data, and related FAIR provenance. It preserves the historic H₂KG namespace, reuses foundational vocabularies where appropriate, and publishes external SKOS alignments separately.
